Chromatory

Privacy

Chromatory runs on your PC. Your models, your paint library, your projects and your photos stay there. There is no account, no sign-in and no telemetry.

What stays on your computer

  • Every model file you load, every render, every project, painting log and photo.
  • Your paint inventory and colour matches.
  • Usage traces the app writes for its own support bundle. They never leave your PC unless you export a support bundle yourself and choose to send it.

When the app talks to the internet

  • Update check. At most once every twelve hours the app asks itch.io whether a newer version exists. The request carries the app's name and channel, nothing about you. You can switch it off in the app and it then never runs.
  • Cloud AI, only if you set it up. Chromatory can use an AI provider you configure with your own key or your own subscription. When you ask it a question, that question and the photos or colours you attach go to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy terms. Nothing is sent until you ask, and a local model on your own machine is supported instead.
  • Your own phone. Pairing a phone happens on your home network with a short-lived code shown on your screen. Chromatory does not relay anything through a server; the phone talks directly to your PC.

What we collect

Nothing. Chromatory has no servers of its own and no analytics. Purchases are handled by the store you bought it from under that store's terms.
